# Changelog (2026-10-01 to 2026-10-04)

## Spotlight

- **Version 2.0.2** — A bug-fix release: uuid refs resolve everywhere they render, publishing exports load, and desktop packages carry the embedding sidecar that semantic search needs. ([6f2c2a0](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/commit/6f2c2a0f659a0b5b1155d31fa42811edb1de0588), [6d5cbe1](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/commit/6d5cbe1438b233a2e19e1a40f060e48d499f5ded))
- **Refs resolve in search, breadcrumbs, and nested page refs** — `[[uuid]]` is the single node-ref form; refs to missing entities stay as broken links instead of raw ids. ([PR #13580](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/pull/13580), [PR #13551](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/pull/13551))
- **Settings survive a broken `config.edn`** — Toggles recover when the file does not parse. ([PR #13566](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/pull/13566))
- **Coming next: the web UI in OCaml** — A draft rewrites the main-thread UI in OCaml/Melange on LUI, beside the OCaml DB worker, the OCaml Electron main process, and CodeMirror 6. ([PR #13531](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/pull/13531), [PR #13411](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/pull/13411), [PR #13472](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/pull/13472), [PR #13389](https://github.com/logseq/logseq/pull/13389))
