# What's new — April 12, 2026

This update is mostly about making things work the way you expect them to. Editing feels more solid, synced graphs are more resilient, and the CLI has grown into a proper tool for power users.

---

## Editing just works now

Undo and redo had a handful of frustrating edge cases — undoing on a local graph did nothing, pasting blocks lost their indentation, inserting a block above another block could end up in the wrong place, and deleting a page sometimes left undo in a broken state. All of those are fixed.

When you undo or redo, your text selection is also restored, so you land back exactly where you were instead of losing your cursor position.

Icons now appear in the right-click block menu, making it easier to scan for the action you want without reading every label.

Date properties can be edited again — that was broken.

---

## Synced and encrypted graphs are more reliable

If you use sync, this release cleans up a class of issues where the app could get into a state where it thought your local graph and the server were out of sync when they weren't. That mismatch caused trouble after undo/redo operations or when reconnecting. It's been substantially stabilized.

If you use an encrypted (E2EE) graph and ever hit a scenario where the graph couldn't be opened after a key problem or a failed download, recovery is now more robust — the app handles missing keys, decrypt failures, and stale key material more gracefully instead of getting stuck.

Large graphs also sync and download faster. Instead of waiting for the entire snapshot before anything happens, data streams in progressively, which means you see your graph sooner.

---

## The CLI is now a proper task and graph management tool

_(This section is for users who use the `logseq` command-line interface.)_

You can now manage tasks entirely from the terminal — list them, filter by status or priority, and update them. A new `graph backup` command lets you back up your graph from a script. `list node` and `list asset` / `upsert asset` round out the set of things you can inspect and modify without opening the app.

There's also a `search` command, a built-in `examples` command that shows you real usage patterns, and `skill install` / `skill show` for discovering and managing CLI skills.

A large pile of bugs were fixed at the same time: page creation failing with "page-not-found", removing a page by name not working correctly, upsert silently doing nothing for partial updates, `--priority` not saving, queries returning fewer results than expected, datetime properties crashing the `show` command, and tilde paths (`~/...`) not being recognized. The CLI should now behave consistently across all these operations.

---

## Faster on larger graphs

Block save, insert, and delete operations are now leaner — the app avoids creating unnecessary intermediate transactions, which translates to snappier editing on graphs with a lot of content. Write operations are also batched more intelligently so the storage layer isn't doing redundant work in the background.
